PROP FIRM GUIDE - TOPSTEP vs FUNDEDNEXT (RB ke liye, 1 Oct 2026)
=================================================================
Rate: 1 USD = ~Rs 96 (1 Oct 2026). Prices badalte rehte hain - khareedne se pehle site pe check karo.
"All-in" = base price + 18% GST (Indian card pe checkout mein lagta hai) + ~3.5% bank forex markup.
Zero-markup forex card (Niyo, Scapia, IDFC First etc.) use karo to ~3.5% bach jayega.


---------------------------------------------------------------
PART A: YEH PROP FIRM KAAM KAISE KARTI HAI (2 minute mein)
---------------------------------------------------------------
1. Aap exam (evaluation) ki fees dete ho. Trading SIMULATED account pe hoti hai.
2. Exam pass = $3,000 profit banao, aur kabhi $2,000 se zyada neeche mat jao.
3. Pass hone pe "funded" account milta hai (abhi bhi simulated). Wahan jo profit banao,
   uska 90% firm aapko ASLI paisa bhejti hai (performance fee).
4. Fail = account khatam, phir se fees / reset.
Sachai: Topstep ke 2025 data mein sirf 16.8% exams pass hue, aur funded logon mein se
sirf 33% ko kabhi payout mila. Hamare backtest (NOISE strategy) mein pass rate 29-45%.

PART B: TOPSTEP (hamara #1)
---------------------------------------------------------------
Firm: 2012 se, Chicago (US). India ELIGIBLE - support (Irene) ne 29 Sep 2026 ko likhit mein bola.

PAISA (50K account, Standard plan):
  Kya                         USD      INR base   INR all-in (approx)
  Combine (exam) per mahina   $49      Rs 4,700   Rs 5,750
  Pass hone pe activation     $149     Rs 14,300  Rs 17,500  (sirf pass hone pe)
  API (guard/bot ke liye)     $14.50   Rs 1,400   Rs 1,700   (per mahina)
  Payout wire fee             $30      payout se katega
  ---------------------------------------------------------
  Shuru mein dena:  ~Rs 5,750 (exam) + Rs 1,700 (API)  = ~Rs 7,500 pehle mahine
  Pass karke payout tak (best case, 1 mahine mein pass): ~Rs 25,000 total
  Fail hua to: har mahine ~Rs 5,750 jab tak pass na ho ya cancel na karo.
  Refund: pehle Combine pe 14 din ke andar (agar pass nahi kiya).

EXAM RULES (Combine 50K):
  - Target: $3,000 profit
  - Max Loss Limit (MLL): $2,000. Din ke close balance se trail karta hai, $50,000 pe lock.
    Chalu trade ka loss bhi gina jata hai - floor chhua to turant account fail.
  - Daily Loss Limit (DLL): $1,000 (hum ON karenge). Hit = us din band, account nahi jata.
  - Consistency: ek din ka profit total ka 55% se zyada hua to target badh jata hai.
    Matlab kam se kam 2 din lagenge.
  - Max size: 5 mini / 50 micro contracts.
  - Sab positions 3:10 PM CT tak band (IST mein raat ~1:40 baje, Nov se ~2:40 baje).

FUNDED (Express) RULES:
  - Balance $0 se shuru, MLL -$2,000.
  - Size: $1,500 se kam = 20 micros, $1,500-2,000 = 30, $2,000+ = 50.
  - Payout ke liye: 5 din $150+ profit (Standard), ya 3 din with ek din <= 40% (Consistency).
  - Ek payout: balance ka 50%, max $2,000 (50K).
  - PEHLE PAYOUT KE BAAD MLL $0 PE FIX - jo balance chhodoge wahi buffer hai.
  - Split: 90% aapka (12 Jan 2026 ke baad join karne walon ke liye).

PAYOUT KAB (Topstep):
  Best case:  Exam 2 din + Express 3-5 din + wire 5-10 business din = ~3-4 hafte.
  Realistic:  Hamare backtest mein median ~1-1.5 mahina per pass, aur 3 mein se ~2 attempts fail.
              Isliye pehla payout 2-4 mahine mein aana normal maano.
  Example: Express mein $1,000 profit -> 50% = $500 nikaalo -> 90% = $450 -> minus $30 = $420
           = ~Rs 40,000 bank mein (SWIFT wire, 5-10 din).

BOT: Combine aur Express mein allowed (official API). Live account mein banned - wahan haath se.

PART E: RULES KAISE SAMBHALENGE (bhoolne se kaise bachein)
---------------------------------------------------------------
4 layer suraksha - ek bhool jao to doosra pakad le:

LAYER 1 - PLATFORM KHUD ROKEGA (TopstepX settings, ek baar set karo):
  - Daily Loss Limit $1,000 (purchase pe ON) -> hit hote hi platform positions band kar deta hai.
  - Personal Daily Loss Limit (TopstepX Risk Settings): $800, "Liquidate and Block" ->
    $800 loss pe platform khud band karke us din trading block.
  - Max contracts limit TopstepX mein bhi set karo (jaise 15 micros).
  - Bracket order default ON: har entry ke saath stop-loss apne aap lage.
  - 3:10 PM CT pe Topstep khud sab band kar deta hai.

LAYER 2 - RULE GUARD (hamara code, aapke Mac pe, har 5 second):
  Command: rbfutg --since <account start date>
  Pehle se alert dega (screen + Mac notification + awaaz):
  - MLL ke paas (50% / 25% buffer bacha)
  - Daily loss limit ke paas (75%)
  - Bina stop ke position
  - Stop itna door ki hit hua to MLL tootega
  - Size limit ke paas / pending order se limit tootegi
  - 3:10 PM CT se 15 min aur 3 min pehle
  - Consistency: ek din ka profit bahut zyada (55% exam / 40% funded) - pehle hi bata dega
  - Express mein: winning days ginti, payout eligible hone pe batayega
  Trade se pehle: rbfutch MNQ 5 20 --since <date>  -> GO / NO-GO

LAYER 3 - AADATEIN (khud ke rules):
  - Har trade pe stop. Bina stop ke kabhi nahi.
  - Ek trade mein max $300 risk.
  - Din ka $800 loss = laptop band.
  - CPI/FOMC/NFP ke time full size nahi (Topstep ka rule).
  - VPN kabhi nahi. Do accounts mein ulta trade (hedging) kabhi nahi.

LAYER 4 - CHECKLIST (har raat trading se pehle, 1 minute):
  [ ] Guard chal raha hai? (rbfutg)
  [ ] Aaj koi badi news (CPI/FOMC/NFP)? Economic calendar dekha?
  [ ] Bracket/stop default ON hai?
  [ ] Mac ka sleep OFF, internet theek?
  [ ] Kal ka loss/profit dekha - aaj kitna room hai?

Yaad rakho: guard ALERT deta hai, rokta nahi. Asli bachav exchange pe laga stop aur
platform ki DLL settings hain. Guard ek dost hai jo kaan mein bolta hai "dhyan se".
